# Stargazing on a Clear Night

On a moonless evening away from city lights, the sky opens into a dense field of distant suns, each pinprick a reminder of the scale beyond our daily routines. A simple pair of binoculars or patience with the naked eye is enough to trace familiar constellations and notice how they wheel slowly overhead. Taking time to look up rarely solves practical problems, yet it often restores a useful sense of perspective.
