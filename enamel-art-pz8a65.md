## Authentication & Condition Reports

- **1971** — Galerie Orientale examination: confirmed period-consistent copper base, wire gauging, and enamel palette typical of Guangzhou export workshops.
- **1998** — Independent conservator report (M. Tissot, Paris): minor surface oxidation on reverse; enamel intact with no restoration detected.
- **2015** — XRF analysis conducted at Université de Lyon: elemental composition consistent with 19th-century lead-based enamel formulations.
