# The Resilience of Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are among the most biodiverse ecosystems on Earth, supporting thousands of species within a delicate, interconnected network of life. These underwater structures face unprecedented challenges from rising ocean temperatures and acidification, yet some corals demonstrate remarkable adaptive capacity through symbiotic relationships with algae and rapid breeding cycles. Conservation efforts focusing on reducing local stressors and protecting marine reserves offer hope for preserving these vital ecosystems for future generations.
