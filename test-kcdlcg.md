# The Fibonacci Sequence

The Fibonacci sequence is a mathematical series where each number is the sum of the two preceding ones, creating a pattern that appears throughout nature in spiral shells, flower petals, and galaxy formations. This elegant sequence was introduced to Western mathematics in the 13th century by Leonardo Fibonacci, though the pattern had been known in Indian mathematics centuries earlier. The ratio between consecutive Fibonacci numbers converges to the golden ratio, a proportion found in art, architecture, and biological growth patterns.
