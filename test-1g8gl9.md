# Morning Light on the Harbor

Small boats sway against their moorings while gulls trace slow arcs above the pilings, and the water holds a glassy calm that breaks only when a wake rolls in from the channel. The tide turns without announcement, yet everyone on the waterfront reads the subtle shift in the rigging's tension and the way fenders begin to kiss the dock with a different rhythm. By mid-morning the fog has lifted, leaving salt on the air and warmth on the planks where crews coil lines and trade quiet plans for the afternoon.
