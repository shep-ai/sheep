# Harbor Fog at Dusk

Coastal fog often forms when moist air moves over cooler water, then drifts inland as a soft, low blanket that muffles sound and shortens sightlines. Sailors and ferry crews treat these evenings with extra care because channels and buoys can disappear into gray within a few minutes. City dwellers sometimes welcome the change of pace, when the waterfront feels quieter and the ordinary skyline turns into a sketch of silhouettes.
