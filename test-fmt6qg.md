# Urban Night Markets

Across many cities, open-air night markets gather locals and tourists under strings of warm lights, turning ordinary streets into lively corridors of sound and scent. Vendors sell grilled food, handmade crafts, and regional produce until late hours, while musicians and performers add a festive backdrop to the crowd. The atmosphere blends commerce and community in a way that daytime shopping rarely matches, and it often becomes the most memorable slice of a visitor’s itinerary.
