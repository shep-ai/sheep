# Stargazing on a Clear Night

A dark sky far from the glow of streetlamps reveals the Milky Way as a faint river of light stretched across the celestial dome, inviting quiet contemplation. Simple binoculars bring distant galaxies and sparkling star clusters into view, turning abstract astronomy into a tangible evening ritual. Taking a slow breath under such a canopy reminds us how small our daily worries are when measured against the vast, patient scale of the cosmos.
