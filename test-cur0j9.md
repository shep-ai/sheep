# Coastal Breezes and Tides

The steady pull of the moon shapes coastlines over centuries, carving bays and depositing sand in gentle arcs along every continental margin. Salt air carries the cries of seabirds above waves that break in predictable rhythms against headlands and sheltered coves. Standing at the edge of the surf with bare feet in cool water, you feel how the ocean both welcomes and humbles every visitor who pauses long enough to listen.
