# The Wonders of Bioluminescence

Bioluminescence is the production and emission of light by living organisms, a fascinating phenomenon found in deep-sea creatures, fireflies, and certain fungi. This natural light show serves various purposes including communication, attracting prey, and defense mechanisms. From the glowing jellyfish of the Mariana Trench to the twinkling fireflies in summer meadows, bioluminescence reveals nature's incredible chemistry at work.
