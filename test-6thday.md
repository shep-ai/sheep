# The Beauty of Autumn Leaves

As the seasons change and temperatures drop, trees across the world put on a spectacular display of color that transforms entire landscapes into a canvas of reds, golds, and oranges. This natural phenomenon occurs because the trees are breaking down the chlorophyll in their leaves, revealing the other pigments that were hidden beneath the green all along. It's a powerful reminder that sometimes the most beautiful transformations happen when we let go of what no longer serves us.
