# The Quiet Life of Urban Bicycles

Bicycles parked along city streets become part of the daily rhythm of commuters running errands and students heading to class. A well-maintained bike can last for decades with only occasional tune-ups, new tires, and chain care when the seasons change. Many cities now invest in dedicated lanes, traffic-calmed corridors, and secure parking to make cycling safer and more inviting for riders of all ages and abilities.
