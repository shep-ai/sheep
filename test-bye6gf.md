# Morning Light in a Small Workshop

Wood grain catches the first daylight through the south window and turns ordinary planks into something worth measuring twice before you commit the saw. A sharp pencil and a steady hand still beat the fanciest laser gadgets when the cut has to land exactly where you marked it, splinters notwithstanding. By noon the air smells of cedar shavings, coffee cooling in a mug, and the quiet satisfaction of joints that actually fit together.
