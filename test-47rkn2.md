# Fresh Bread at Dawn

Artisan bakers often start mixing dough while most people are still asleep, chasing the delicate window when yeast activity peaks and ovens are still cooling from the prior bake. The aroma of a crust caramelizing in a hot oven has drawn neighbors to small bakeries for centuries, long before social media could spread weekend recommendations. A simple loaf connects growers, millers, and families at breakfast tables without much fanfare, yet it quietly anchors local economies block by block.
