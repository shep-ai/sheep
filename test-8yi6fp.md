# Quiet Mornings in the Workshop

Woodworking shops often smell of sawdust and linseed oil long before the first cut of the day, when light slants across the bench and hand tools wait in neat rows. Careful measurement and dry-fitting save more time than rushing through a joint that will not quite close under clamp pressure. Patience at the bench turns rough lumber into furniture that can last for generations if the grain is honored and the finish is applied with care.
