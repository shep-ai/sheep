# Cloud Computing Timeline

Cloud computing emerged in the 2000s as companies like AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform began offering on-demand computing resources and storage services. Major milestones include the launch of AWS EC2 in 2006, the rise of containerization with Docker in 2013, and the widespread adoption of serverless computing throughout the 2010s. Today, cloud infrastructure powers most of the internet and has become essential for digital transformation across industries.
