# Urban Composting at Home

Composting kitchen scraps in a small lidded bin turns everyday organic waste into dark, crumbly soil amendment you can use on houseplants or balcony containers without hauling bags from the store. Over a few weeks, worms and aerobic microorganisms digest vegetable peels, coffee grounds, crushed eggshells, and torn paper as long as you avoid meat, dairy, and oily leftovers. Keeping a loose balance of nitrogen-rich greens and carbon-heavy browns prevents slimy smells and helps the decomposition stay steady through changing seasons.
