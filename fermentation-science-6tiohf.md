# Fermentation Science Troubleshooting Guide

## Common Problems and Solutions

### Fermentation Won't Start

**Symptoms:** No bubbling, no activity after 24–48 hours.

- Check yeast viability — use fresh, properly stored yeast
- Verify temperature is in the optimal range (18–26°C for most ale yeasts)
- Ensure sugar concentration (OG) is not too high (>1.120 can stress yeast)
- Confirm no sanitizer residue killed the yeast

### Fermentation Stopped Too Early

**Symptoms:** Final gravity is higher than expected; sweetness remains.

- Check temperature — too cold stalls yeast activity
- Rouse the yeast by gently swirling the vessel
- Pitch additional active yeast starter
- Ensure enough nutrients are present (add yeast nutrient if needed)

### Off-Flavors

| Flavor | Likely Cause | Fix |
|--------|-------------|-----|
| Sulfur / rotten egg | Stressed yeast, lack of nutrients | Add nutrients, warm slightly, rouse |
| Vinegar (acetic acid) | Acetobacter contamination from oxygen | Minimize air exposure; sanitize better |
| Butter / butterscotch | Diacetyl from yeast | Raise temp 2–3°C for a diacetyl rest |
| Medicinal / plastic | Chloramine in water or wild yeast | Use filtered/campden-treated water |
| Sour / lactic | Lactobacillus contamination | Improve sanitation; use acidulated malt intentionally |

### Cloudy/Hazy Product (Unexpected)

- Chill the batch — cold crashing drops yeast and proteins
- Add fining agents (gelatin, bentonite, Irish moss)
- Allow more conditioning time
- Check water chemistry (high temporary hardness causes haze)

### Overcarbonation / Bottle Bombs

- Ensure fermentation is fully complete before packaging
- Verify priming sugar calculation (use weight, not volume)
- Maintain consistent conditioning temperature
- Check for wild yeast or bacterial refermentation

### Flat Product

- Confirm priming sugar was added and mixed evenly
- Check bottle caps/seals for leaks
- Condition at a warmer temperature (18–22°C)
- Allow more time — carbonation can take 2–4 weeks

## Key Fermentation Parameters

| Parameter | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Temperature | 18–26°C | Strain-dependent |
| pH | 4.0–5.5 | Lower inhibits spoilage organisms |
| OG | 1.040–1.100 | Higher = longer fermentation |
| Pitch rate | 0.75–1.5M cells/mL/°P | Under-pitching causes stress |

## Prevention Checklist

- [ ] Sanitize all equipment thoroughly
- [ ] Use quality, fresh ingredients
- [ ] Control fermentation temperature
- [ ] Minimize oxygen exposure post-fermentation
- [ ] Monitor gravity readings to confirm completion
