# Stargazing on Clear Autumn Nights

On a clear autumn night, the Milky Way stretches across the sky like spilled sugar, and a borrowed park bench becomes a front-row seat to the universe. Binoculars reveal star clusters and nebulae that the naked eye only hints at, turning a casual glance into a short tour of deep space. Taking even a few minutes to look up can reset the mind after a long day of screens, meetings, and scrolling, leaving you with a quieter sense of scale.
