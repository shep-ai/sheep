# Urban Night Walks

Walking through a quiet city after dark reveals rhythms you rarely notice in daylight, from the soft hum of streetlights to the distant pulse of traffic and the occasional murmur of late-night conversations. A short stroll can clear the mind and make familiar streets feel gently unfamiliar, as if the map had been redrawn in softer ink. Many people find that these unhurried circuits become a dependable way to unwind before sleep, without the pressure of a destination or a clock.
