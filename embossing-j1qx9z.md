# Embossing Comparison

A brief comparison of common embossing techniques used in printing and paper craft. Each method offers distinct visual and tactile effects suited to different applications.

| Technique | Direction | Materials | Best For |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Blind embossing | Raised | Paper only | Elegant, minimalist branding |
| Debossing | Recessed | Paper only | Subtle, refined impressions |
| Heat/foil embossing | Raised + metallic | Ink, foil, powder | Certificates, invitations |
