# Honey Bee Dance: Stakeholder Map

## Overview

The honey bee waggle dance is a figure-eight movement performed by forager bees to communicate the direction and distance of food sources, water, or nest sites to other colony members.

---

## Stakeholders

### 1. Forager Bee (Dancer)
- **Role:** Performs the waggle dance after returning from a resource site
- **Interest:** Recruits nestmates to maximize colony foraging efficiency
- **Actions:** Encodes distance (duration of waggle run) and direction (angle relative to sun) in the dance

### 2. Follower Bees (Audience)
- **Role:** Observe and decode the dance inside the hive
- **Interest:** Locate the resource without scouting independently
- **Actions:** Follow dance circuits, sample odor cues, then fly to the indicated site

### 3. Queen Bee
- **Role:** Reproductive head of the colony
- **Interest:** Colony growth and survival depend on successful foraging
- **Actions:** Indirectly benefits; dance communication sustains the worker population she relies on

### 4. Scout Bees
- **Role:** Discover new food sources or nest sites before dancing
- **Interest:** Evaluating resource quality to decide whether to dance at all
- **Actions:** Assess quantity, quality, and distance; dance vigor reflects site value

### 5. Colony (Superorganism)
- **Role:** Collective decision-maker
- **Interest:** Optimizing resource intake and swarm site selection
- **Actions:** Aggregates dance information to achieve quorum decisions (especially during swarming)

---

## External Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | Relationship to Dance |
|-------------|----------|-----------------------|
| Flowering Plants | Pollination | Benefit from targeted bee visits driven by dance recruitment |
| Beekeepers | Hive productivity | Monitor foraging success; hive placement affects dance-encoded distances |
| Researchers | Understanding animal communication | Study dance as a model of symbolic language and collective intelligence |
| Ecosystem | Biodiversity | Pollination driven by dance efficiency supports plant diversity |

---

## Key Dependencies

- **Sun compass:** Dance direction is calibrated to the sun's position; overcast days degrade accuracy
- **Hive substrate:** Vertical comb surface is required for gravity-referenced dance orientation
- **Nestmate attention:** Dance is only effective if follower bees are present and attentive
