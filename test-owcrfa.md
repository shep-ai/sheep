# Morning Light on the Windowsill

Sunlight spills across the sill long before the rest of the house stirs, catching dust in slow, golden arcs that seem to hang in the still air. A potted herb tilts toward the glass as if remembering warmer seasons beyond the pane, its leaves trembling when a draft slips through the frame. In that quiet hour, the day feels unwritten and full of small, possible beginnings you can almost hold in your hand.
