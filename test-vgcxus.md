# The Art of Fermentation

Fermentation is an ancient process that has been used for thousands of years to preserve food and create delicious beverages like wine, beer, and kombucha. The process relies on beneficial microorganisms that break down sugars and other compounds, producing lactic acid or alcohol as byproducts. Beyond preservation, fermentation enhances nutritional value and creates complex flavors that make fermented foods sought after by chefs and food enthusiasts worldwide.
