# The Art of Stargazing

Stargazing is one of humanity's oldest pastimes, offering a humbling perspective on our place in the cosmos. On a clear night away from city lights, you can see thousands of stars with the naked eye, each one a potential sun with its own planetary systems. Whether you're an amateur astronomer with a telescope or simply someone who enjoys looking up at the night sky, stargazing connects us to the vastness of the universe and reminds us of how small we truly are.
