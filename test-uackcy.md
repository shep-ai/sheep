# Morning Light in the City

Urban mornings have a rhythm of their own, as commuters and shopkeepers share the same quiet streets before the day grows loud and hurried. Soft sunlight on glass and steel can make even routine commutes feel briefly cinematic, as if the ordinary block were borrowed from a film set. Taking a moment to notice that light, the steam from a coffee cup, and the first voices on the sidewalk can be a small but meaningful way to start the day with intention.
