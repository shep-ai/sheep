# The Beauty of Autumn Leaves

Autumn leaves display some of nature's most vibrant colors as trees prepare for winter by withdrawing nutrients from their foliage. The transformation from green to brilliant shades of red, orange, and gold happens because chlorophyll breaks down, revealing other pigments like anthocyanins and carotenoids that were always present. This seasonal spectacle attracts visitors from around the world who come to witness the fleeting display of natural beauty.
