# The Magic of Autumn Leaves

Autumn leaves transform trees into spectacular displays of red, orange, and gold as chlorophyll breaks down and reveals the pigments beneath. This process, called senescence, happens when days grow shorter and temperatures drop, signaling trees to stop producing the green pigment that masks other colors. The brilliant colors we admire are actually present year-round but hidden by chlorophyll, making autumn a breathtaking reminder of nature's hidden beauty.
