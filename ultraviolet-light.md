---

## Mouthfeel & Texture

- **Body:** Thin, high-acid, with a persistent metallic tingle
- **Finish:** Long, drying — like biting a lemon rind under fluorescent light
- **Aftertaste:** Phantom warmth on the palate; faint sulfur echo

|-----------------------|------------------------------------------|

| Grapefruit pith       | Matching bitterness + citrus acid        |
| Tonic water           | Quinine resonance (UV fluoresces tonic)  |

---

## Sommelier Notes

UV light presents as an **uncompromising, high-frequency experience** — no sweetness,
no fat, no warmth. It strips rather than enriches. Best appreciated in small doses;
prolonged exposure overwhelms every other note on the palate.

**Overall rating:** ★★★☆☆ — bracing and clarifying, but not a sipper.
