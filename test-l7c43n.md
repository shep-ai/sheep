# The Mysteries of Ocean Bioluminescence

Bioluminescence in the ocean creates one of nature's most spectacular light shows, where countless organisms from jellyfish to deep-sea fish produce their own light through chemical reactions. This ethereal glow serves many purposes including attracting prey, communicating with mates, and confusing predators in the dark depths where sunlight never reaches. Scientists estimate that over 90% of deep-sea creatures produce bioluminescence, making it one of the most common characteristics of marine life.
