# The Art of Procrastination

Procrastination is often misunderstood as laziness, but it's actually a complex emotional regulation problem where we avoid tasks that make us anxious or bored. The best strategy is not to fight it with willpower, but to break tasks into smaller, less intimidating pieces and pair them with something enjoyable. Sometimes the hardest part is just starting—once you do, momentum takes over and the task becomes less daunting than you initially thought.
