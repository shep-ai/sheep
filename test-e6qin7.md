# Morning Light on Quiet Water

Dawn over a still lake turns mist into soft gold and reminds us how brief and beautiful calm can be in a world that rarely slows down. Birds begin their calls while the surface holds a mirror of the waking sky, and ripples arrive only when a breeze finally stirs. Taking a moment to notice such mornings can reset the mind before the day’s demands arrive, much like drawing a full breath before a long climb.
