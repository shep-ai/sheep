# The Strength of Resilience

Resilience is the capacity to recover and adapt in the face of adversity, setback, or change, a quality that defines both individuals and organizations capable of thriving amid uncertainty. This strength is not born from avoiding challenges but from learning to navigate them with flexibility, persistence, and the wisdom to know when to adjust course. By cultivating resilience, we develop the foundation for sustainable growth and the ability to transform obstacles into opportunities for meaningful development.
