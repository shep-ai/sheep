# Urban Bicycling in Spring

Riding a bicycle through city streets is one of the quickest ways to notice the seasons changing around you, from bare branches to full canopies in just a few weeks. Spring brings longer daylight, milder air, and the first green leaves along familiar routes that commuters and weekend riders share. Many people find that a short ride can turn daily travel into a light workout, a quieter commute than driving, and a small daily chance to clear their head before work begins.
