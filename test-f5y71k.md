# Aurora Borealis and Magnetic Phenomena

The aurora borealis, commonly known as the northern lights, is a spectacular natural phenomenon caused by charged particles from the sun interacting with Earth's magnetic field. When solar wind collides with our planet's magnetosphere, it creates brilliant curtains of green, purple, and red light that dance across the night sky in high-latitude regions. This awe-inspiring display demonstrates the invisible forces that constantly shape our environment and remind us of our planet's dynamic relationship with the sun.
