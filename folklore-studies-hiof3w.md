# Top 10 Folklore Studies Essentials

Folklore studies examines oral traditions, customs, and beliefs passed through generations. Here are ten key concepts every student of the field should know.

1. Oral tradition — stories, songs, and sayings transmitted by word of mouth
2. Myth — sacred narratives explaining origins and cosmology
3. Legend — stories presented as historically true
4. Folktale — fictional narratives shared for entertainment
5. Custom — habitual practices within a community
6. Material culture — artifacts, crafts, and built environments
7. Performance — folklore as enacted practice
8. Context — social setting in which folklore is shared
9. Variation — regional and individual differences in tradition
10. Collection — documenting and preserving folk expressions
