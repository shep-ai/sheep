# Urban Parks and Quiet Mornings

City parks offer rare pockets of calm where birdsong and tree shade soften the noise of traffic and everyday deadlines. A short walk among winding paths, open lawns, and simple benches can reset attention before the workday gathers speed and meetings stack up. Many people find that even fifteen minutes outdoors improves mood and focus for hours afterward, which is why planners increasingly weave green space into dense neighborhoods.
