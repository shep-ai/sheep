# Morning light on quiet water

Sunrise paints the river in soft gold while mist lifts from the surface and the cool air carries the faint scent of wet stone along the bank. A heron stands motionless at the water’s edge, patient as the current, with only the slow turn of its head betraying a hunter’s quiet attention. Small ripples mark the passage of a fish beneath the bridge, and the day begins without hurry, as if the world has agreed to move gently for a little while longer.
