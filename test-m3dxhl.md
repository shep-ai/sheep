# Morning Light on Quiet Water

Dawn breaks slowly over the still surface of the lake, turning mist into pale gold while the eastern sky gathers color in thin, patient layers. A single heron stands motionless at the edge of the reeds, its reflection barely rippling when a breeze threads through the cattails. In moments like these, the boundary between watching the world and feeling part of it seems to soften until it is hard to say where the shoreline ends and the morning begins.
