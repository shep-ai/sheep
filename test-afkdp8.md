# Quiet Corners of the Library

Public libraries reward slow browsing more than quick answers, with shelves that invite pleasant detours through subjects you had not planned to explore and tangents that feel more informative than distraction. The subdued hush between stacks makes it easier to concentrate on a single page without the constant tug of notifications and competing tabs on a laptop screen. Many readers discover that a borrowed book carries a soft deadline that gently nudges them to finish what a permanent home shelf might happily postpone for months on end.
