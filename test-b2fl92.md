# The Northern Lights

The Northern Lights, or Aurora Borealis, are a natural phenomenon caused by solar wind particles interacting with Earth's magnetic field and atmosphere. These magnificent displays of green, purple, and red light create some of the most breathtaking spectacles in nature, inspiring awe in observers across the Arctic regions. Witnessing the Aurora Borealis is a bucket-list experience for many travelers seeking to connect with the raw beauty of our planet.
