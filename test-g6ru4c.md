## Bioluminescence in the Ocean

Bioluminescence is the production and emission of light by living organisms, a phenomenon found most commonly in deep-sea creatures, fireflies, and certain fungi. These organisms use chemical reactions within their bodies to create light, which they employ for various purposes including attracting mates, luring prey, and communicating with other members of their species. This magical light display in the darkness of the ocean depths remains one of nature's most fascinating visual spectacles.
