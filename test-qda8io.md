# The Wonders of Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are some of the most vibrant and diverse ecosystems on Earth, supporting an incredible array of marine life despite covering less than one percent of the ocean floor. These underwater structures are built by tiny organisms called coral polyps that work together to create the colorful gardens we marvel at. Unfortunately, many coral reefs are under threat from climate change, pollution, and human activities, making their protection crucial for the survival of countless species that depend on them.
