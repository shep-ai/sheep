# Morning Light on the Harbor

Sunrise paints the waterfront in soft color while the tide eases against the pilings in a steady rhythm that has repeated here for decades. Small boats shift at their moorings, rigging whispers in the breeze, and gulls wheel overhead in slow, patient arcs that mirror the turning of the day. On mornings like this, the city’s hurry feels distant, replaced by the simple, grounding work of wind, water, and light.
