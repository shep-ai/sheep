# Morning Light on a Quiet Harbor

Small boats rested at anchor while gulls traced lazy circles above the water, and the tide whispered against pilings that had known decades of salt and rope. The harbor woke slowly, with fishermen checking their nets and shopkeepers rolling up shutters along the pier while radios crackled with weather and tide tables. By the time the sun cleared the headland, the town had settled into a rhythm of nets, coffee, and salt air that would carry the working waterfront through another unhurried day.
