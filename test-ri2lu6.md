# The Art of Procrastination

Procrastination is often misunderstood as laziness, but it's actually a complex emotional regulation strategy where we prioritize immediate relief over long-term goals. The irony is that delaying tasks typically increases stress and reduces the quality of our work, creating a vicious cycle that's surprisingly difficult to break. Understanding the root causes of our procrastination habits—whether perfectionism, fear of failure, or simple lack of interest—is the first step toward developing healthier work patterns.
