# Northern Lights

The Aurora Borealis, commonly known as the Northern Lights, is a natural light display in Earth's sky that occurs in high-latitude regions. This spectacular phenomenon is caused by the collision of charged particles from the solar wind with atoms in Earth's atmosphere, creating ribbons of green, red, and violet light. Viewing the Northern Lights has become a bucket-list experience for many travelers who journey to Arctic regions during winter months to witness this breathtaking celestial dance.
