# The Art of Procrastination

Procrastination is often misunderstood as mere laziness, but it's actually a complex emotional regulation strategy where people delay tasks to avoid negative feelings. Studies show that chronic procrastinators experience higher stress levels and poorer health outcomes, yet they continue the cycle because the immediate relief outweighs future consequences. Breaking free from procrastination requires understanding the emotions driving the delay and developing compassionate self-management techniques rather than relying on willpower alone.
