# Morning Light on the Harbor

Sunrise paints the water in soft gold while small boats shift at their moorings and rigging taps a quiet rhythm against the masts. Seabirds trace lazy arcs above the pilings, and the tide pulls slowly toward the open sea, carrying the smell of salt and kelp across the docks. Days that begin beside quiet water often feel simpler than they really are, even when the week ahead is crowded with errands and deadlines.
