# The Beauty of Northern Lights

The aurora borealis, commonly known as the Northern Lights, is a natural light display in Earth's sky that occurs primarily in high-latitude regions. These mesmerizing green, purple, and pink curtains of light are caused by collisions between solar wind and magnetospheric charged particles, creating one of nature's most spectacular phenomena. Witnessing the Northern Lights dancing across an Arctic sky is considered a once-in-a-lifetime experience by travelers and scientists alike.
