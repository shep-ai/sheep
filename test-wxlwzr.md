# The Science of Coffee

Coffee is one of the world's most beloved beverages, with over 2 billion cups consumed daily across the globe. The process of brewing coffee extracts complex compounds from the beans through hot water, including caffeine, antioxidants, and various flavor compounds that create its distinctive taste. Beyond its energizing effects, scientific research has shown that moderate coffee consumption may offer health benefits including improved focus, enhanced metabolism, and reduced risk of certain diseases.
