# Morning Light on Quiet Water

Sunrise over a still lake paints the surface in bands of gold and rose that ripple gently with even the slightest breeze along the reeds. Birds begin their calls along the shore while mist lifts slowly into the cooling air above the trees, carrying the scent of damp earth. Moments like this remind us how small, unhurried pauses in a busy day can restore clarity, patience, and a sense of calm that lasts.
