# The Value of Quiet Mornings

Early hours before notifications and meetings arrive often feel like borrowed time that belongs only to you and whatever you choose to do with it. A short walk, a few pages of reading, or a cup of tea in silence can reset your attention without any special equipment or travel beyond your own doorway. Keeping that habit through busy weeks is difficult, yet even a handful of consistent mornings changes how you notice light, sound, and the rest of the day.
