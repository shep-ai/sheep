# The Quiet Hour Before Dawn

The hour before sunrise often feels suspended between night and day, when the world holds its breath and clocks seem to slow for anyone awake to notice. Birds begin to stir in the hedgerows while streetlights still hum against a paleening sky, and the first commuters have not yet filled the roads with noise or urgency. In that liminal space, small worries can shrink, perspective returns quietly, and the day ahead feels briefly tractable rather than overwhelming.
