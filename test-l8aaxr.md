# Quantum Computing

Quantum computers leverage quantum mechanical phenomena such as superposition and entanglement to process information in fundamentally different ways than classical computers. While classical bits exist in either 0 or 1 states, quantum bits (qubits) can exist in superposition, allowing quantum computers to explore multiple solution paths simultaneously. This extraordinary computational advantage promises to revolutionize fields like cryptography, drug discovery, and optimization problems that are intractable for classical systems.
