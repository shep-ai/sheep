# Undersea Cables Myths Busted

Contrary to popular belief, undersea cables don't cause shark attacks—sharks are more interested in actual prey than in fiber optic infrastructure. The cables are incredibly resilient, protected by multiple armor layers and insulation, making them far safer than most surface infrastructure. The real myth worth debunking is that they're fragile; modern submarine cables handle decades of reliable service and support over 99% of all international data traffic.
