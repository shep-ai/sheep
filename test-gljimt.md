# Notes on Spring Rain

Spring rain softens garden soil and signals plants to resume active growth after winter dormancy, when roots once again draw moisture and nutrients toward new leaves. Puddles along neighborhood paths remind walkers to slow down and notice buds opening on branches overhead, each droplet catching light like a tiny lens. Even a brief shower can leave the air smelling clean and renewed, as if the whole block had taken a deep breath together before the afternoon sun returns.
