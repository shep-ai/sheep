# Urban Birdwatching

Birdwatching in cities reveals surprising species that adapt to concrete, traffic noise, and unpredictable weather along narrow green corridors. Small neighborhood parks and restored riverbanks become migration stopovers for warblers, finches, and occasional raptors passing overhead. Even a modest balcony feeder filled with sunflower seeds can attract regular visitors who brighten an otherwise ordinary weekday morning before work.
