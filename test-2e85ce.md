# Coastal Fog at Dawn

Sea fog rolls in from the ocean and softens every edge of the pier until distant lights look like pale halos in the damp air. Fishermen nod to each other without speaking, trusting the routine more than the morning weather report on the crackling radio. By the time the sun burns through, the boards are warm underfoot and the bay smells of salt, diesel, and the faint sweetness of bait buckets left in the shade.
