# The Wonders of Bioluminescence

Bioluminescence is the production of light by living organisms through a chemical reaction within their cells. This fascinating phenomenon occurs in many creatures, from fireflies and deep-sea fish to certain fungi and bacteria, allowing them to communicate, hunt, and attract mates in dark environments. The efficiency of bioluminescence is remarkable—it produces almost no heat, making it far more efficient than human-made incandescent lighting.
