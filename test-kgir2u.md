# Coastal Fog and Tide Pools

When the marine layer rolls in well before dawn, the entire shoreline softens until only vague shapes remain visible through the pearl-gray curtain of mist. Tide pools along the basalt shelves hold miniature worlds where green anemones pulse gently and hermit crabs negotiate shell trades in patient, deliberate choreography. As the fog finally lifts toward midday, warm sunlight catches every wet stone and the glistening surfaces briefly mirror strips of pale blue sky above the horizon.
