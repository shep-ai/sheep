# Morning Light on the Harbor

The first rays of sun paint the water in soft gold while quiet waves lap against the pilings, and the air carries a faint salt breeze that wakes the senses before the bustle of the day. Seabirds wheel overhead in lazy arcs, and small boats rock gently at their moorings as rigging taps a patient rhythm against aluminum masts. It is a peaceful moment before engines cough to life, radios crackle, and the harbor's work begins in earnest.
