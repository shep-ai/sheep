# Morning Light on Quiet Waters

Sunrise paints the calm surface of the lake in soft gold and rose, while mist rises in thin spirals along the reed-lined shore and the air carries a faint scent of damp earth. Small birds call from the shallows, and a lone rowboat drifts gently toward the eastern light as ripples widen behind it. Days that begin like this remind us that stillness and motion can share the same quiet moment, and that patience often rewards anyone willing to watch the world wake.
