# The Art of Tea Brewing

Brewing the perfect cup of tea requires attention to water temperature, steep time, and leaf quality. Different tea varieties demand different brewing conditions; for example, delicate green teas prefer cooler water around 160-180°F, while robust black teas thrive in boiling water. The ritual of tea brewing has been perfected over centuries across cultures, transforming a simple beverage into an experience of mindfulness and relaxation.
