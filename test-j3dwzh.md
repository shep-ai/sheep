# Octopus Intelligence

Octopuses are among the most intelligent invertebrates on Earth, possessing distributed neural networks where about two-thirds of their neurons reside in their arms rather than their brain. This unique neurological structure allows them to perform complex problem-solving tasks, manipulate objects with remarkable dexterity, and even use tools to accomplish their goals. Their remarkable ability to learn by observation and adapt quickly to new environments demonstrates cognitive capabilities that rival some vertebrate species.
