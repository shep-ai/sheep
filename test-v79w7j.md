# The Art of Procrastination

Procrastination is often misunderstood as laziness, but it's actually a complex emotional regulation strategy where we delay tasks to avoid negative feelings. Studies show that about 20% of people are chronic procrastinators, struggling with self-discipline and time management on a daily basis. The key to overcoming procrastination is breaking tasks into smaller, manageable steps and addressing the underlying emotions that trigger avoidance behaviors.
