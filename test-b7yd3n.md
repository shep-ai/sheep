# The Art of Coffee Brewing

Brewing the perfect cup of coffee is both a science and an art that requires attention to detail and quality ingredients. The optimal water temperature should be between 195-205°F, and the grind size should be matched to your brewing method to extract the best flavors. Whether you prefer pour-over, French press, or espresso, the key to great coffee lies in freshly roasted beans, proper proportions, and patience.
