# The Wonders of Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are among the most vibrant and biodiverse ecosystems on Earth, supporting thousands of species despite covering less than 1% of the ocean floor. These underwater structures are built by tiny organisms called coral polyps that form symbiotic relationships with algae, creating a foundation for fish, crustaceans, and countless other marine animals to thrive. Unfortunately, rising ocean temperatures and acidification threaten these delicate ecosystems, making conservation efforts critical to preserve their beauty and ecological importance.
