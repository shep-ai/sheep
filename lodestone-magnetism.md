# Lodestone Magnetism: Restoration Guide

## What Is a Lodestone?

A lodestone is a naturally magnetized piece of magnetite (Fe₃O₄), one of the few minerals found magnetized in nature. Its magnetism arises from magnetic domains aligned by lightning strikes or Earth's geomagnetic field during formation.

---

## Assessing Magnetism Strength

Before restoring, measure the current magnetic strength:

- Use a **gaussmeter** to record baseline field strength (in Gauss or mT).
- Test attraction force with small iron filings or a compass needle.
- Note any asymmetry — one pole may be weaker than the other.

---

## Causes of Demagnetization

| Cause | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Heat above 580°C (Curie point) | Full demagnetization |
| Mechanical shock / dropping | Partial domain disruption |
| Opposing magnetic field | Domain reversal or weakening |
| Age / oxidation | Gradual surface weakening |

---

## Restoration Methods

### 1. Stroking Method (Traditional)
- Use a strong permanent magnet (neodymium preferred).
- Stroke from center to pole tip in one direction only — do **not** reverse.
- Repeat 20–30 strokes per pole, alternating poles evenly.
- Re-test with gaussmeter between sessions.

### 2. Electromagnetic Remagnetization
- Place the lodestone inside a solenoid coil.
- Apply a DC pulse (brief high-current burst) aligned with the stone's original pole orientation.
- Use polarity markers made before treatment to preserve original orientation.

### 3. Earth-Field Alignment (Passive)
- Orient the lodestone along the magnetic north–south axis.
- Leave undisturbed for weeks to months.
- Effective only for mild demagnetization.

---

## Post-Restoration Care

- Store with **keeper bars** (iron bars bridging the poles) to maintain domain alignment.
- Avoid heat, strong opposing fields, and impact.
- Re-test strength every 6–12 months.
- Keep away from electronics and credit cards.

---

## Safety Notes

- Neodymium magnets used in restoration are powerful — handle with care to avoid pinch injuries.
- Do not heat a lodestone to remagnetize; this destroys crystalline structure permanently.
