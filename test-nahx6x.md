# The Art of Procrastination

Procrastination is often misunderstood as mere laziness, but it's actually a complex emotional regulation strategy where we avoid tasks that make us anxious or overwhelmed. The irony of procrastination is that delaying work typically creates more stress and pressure, resulting in a vicious cycle that becomes harder to break. The key to overcoming procrastination is understanding what emotions the task triggers and addressing those feelings directly rather than avoiding the task itself.
