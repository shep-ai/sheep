# The Wonders of Marine Bioluminescence

Marine bioluminescence is one of nature's most spectacular phenomena, occurring when deep-sea creatures produce their own light through chemical reactions. This glowing display serves many purposes, from attracting prey and mates to confusing predators in the darkest depths of the ocean. Scientists estimate that nearly 90% of deep-sea creatures possess some form of bioluminescence, making it an essential adaptation for survival in an environment where sunlight never reaches.
