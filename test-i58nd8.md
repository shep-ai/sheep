# Morning Light on Quiet Water

Dawn breaks slowly over a still lake, turning mist into bands of pale gold that cling to the far shore. A lone heron stands in the shallows, patient as the water cools the last heat of night and the first breeze lifts the reeds. In that hush, small ripples ring outward whenever a fish rises, reminding anyone watching how gentle motion can change a whole surface before the day fully arrives.
