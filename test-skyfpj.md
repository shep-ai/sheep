# Morning Light on Quiet Water

Sunrise over a still lake turns the surface into a sheet of pale gold that ripples only when a bird lands nearby, and for a few minutes the boundary between sky and water seems to dissolve into a single luminous band. Small waves carry that light toward the shore, where reeds bend in a breeze too gentle to name, and footprints from yesterday soften at the waterline until they are barely visible. By mid-morning the glare softens, and the water holds a clear, patient reflection of the sky while dragonflies trace quick, hesitant paths above the shallows.
