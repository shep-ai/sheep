# The Art of Stargazing

Looking up at the night sky has captivated humans for thousands of years, offering a window into the vastness of the universe. Stargazing doesn't require expensive telescopes or deep astronomical knowledge—just an open sky, clear weather, and a sense of wonder. Whether you're identifying constellations, watching meteor showers, or simply contemplating your place in the cosmos, the practice connects you to something far greater than yourself.
