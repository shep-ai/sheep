# Common Mistakes About Tectonic Plates

Many people mistakenly believe that tectonic plates move continuously and smoothly, when in reality they move in jerky motions measured in centimeters per year. A common misconception is that earthquakes only occur along visible plate boundaries, but stress can build up far inland from these boundaries, causing unexpected seismic activity. Finally, people often underestimate how slowly plates move—the same speed at which fingernails grow—leading them to think plate tectonics don't significantly shape our landscape within human timescales.
