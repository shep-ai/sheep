# Urban Night Walks

Walking through a quiet city after dark reframes familiar streets as softer, more intimate spaces where routine landmarks take on a different silhouette. Streetlights and shop windows cast pools of warm color that invite slower attention than daylight commutes usually allow, drawing the eye to textures and details that rush-hour glances miss. Even a modest loop around a single block can feel like a quiet reset before you return indoors, closing the door on the hum of traffic and neon.
