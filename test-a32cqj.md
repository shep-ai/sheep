# Urban Gardening on a Windowsill

Growing herbs and small vegetables on a sunny windowsill brings fresh flavors into city kitchens without needing a yard. A few pots with good drainage, quality potting mix, and consistent watering are enough to keep parsley, basil, or cherry tomatoes thriving through the season. Harvesting a handful of leaves for dinner connects daily cooking to the quiet pleasure of watching something you planted grow.
