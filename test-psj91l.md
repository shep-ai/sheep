# Evening Walks in the City

Taking a leisurely walk through the city after dark can feel like visiting an entirely different neighborhood than the one you know by daylight. Streetlights cast warm pools of light on the pavement, and the usual daytime noise fades into a quieter, more reflective rhythm. Small sensory details—the smell of rain on concrete, a breeze between buildings, or a distant train—become easier to notice once the crowds have thinned out.
