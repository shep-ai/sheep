# Stargazing in the High Desert

When night falls over the high desert, the Milky Way stretches from horizon to horizon like spilled salt on black velvet. Coyotes call in the distance while campers share quiet stories beside cooling embers, and the air turns cool enough for jackets and wool blankets. Even a few minutes under that sky reminds you how small daily worries can feel against something so vast, ancient, and quietly indifferent to human schedules.
