# The Wonders of Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are among the most vibrant and biodiverse ecosystems on Earth, supporting over 25% of all known marine species despite covering less than 1% of the ocean floor. These underwater structures are built by tiny organisms called polyps that work together to create massive limestone formations that protect countless fish, crustaceans, and other marine life. Sadly, coral reefs face serious threats from climate change, pollution, and overfishing, making conservation efforts crucial for preserving these magical underwater worlds.
