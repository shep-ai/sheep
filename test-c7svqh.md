# Morning Light on Quiet Water

Sunrise paints the lake in soft bands of amber and rose while mist drifts above the surface in slow, patient curls that seem to hold the night’s last breath. Small ripples answer the first breeze of the day, carrying the scent of pine from the far shore and the faint call of a bird still deciding where to land. In that still hour, the world feels briefly arranged for listening rather than rushing, as if the day were offering a gentle pause before the ordinary noise returns.
