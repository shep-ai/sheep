# Morning Light on the Harbor

Sunrise paints the water in soft gold while the boats sway gently at their moorings along the wooden pier, and a thin mist lifts where the tide meets the pilings. A few gulls wheel overhead and call to one another, and the city stirs behind the warehouses with the first traffic and the faint rumble of a coffee grinder opening for the day. On mornings like this, the harbor feels wide open and unhurried, as if it has granted everyone a quiet extra minute before the ordinary rush begins.
