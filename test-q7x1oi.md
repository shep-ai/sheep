# The Wonders of Deep Ocean Bioluminescence

The deep ocean is home to creatures that produce their own light through a process called bioluminescence, creating a spectacular light show in the pitch-black depths. This natural illumination serves multiple purposes, from attracting prey and communicating with other organisms to providing camouflage in an environment where any light from above would make them visible to predators. Scientists estimate that up to 90% of deep-sea creatures are bioluminescent, making it one of the most common biological phenomena on Earth.
