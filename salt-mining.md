# Salt Mining: Durability Ranking

A guide to the relative durability of materials, equipment, and structures used in salt mining environments.

---

## Durability Tiers

Salt is highly corrosive to metals, concrete, and organic materials. The rankings below reflect resistance to salt exposure, humidity, and mechanical wear.

### Tier 1 — Excellent Durability

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| HDPE (plastic) conveyor liners | Non-reactive, resists salt and moisture |
| Fiberglass-reinforced plastic (FRP) | Used for tanks, grating, and enclosures |
| Titanium fittings | Exceptional corrosion resistance, high cost |
| Ceramic coatings | Protect metal surfaces in high-contact zones |

### Tier 2 — Good Durability

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Stainless steel (316L grade) | Strong salt resistance; avoid crevice joints |
| Epoxy-coated rebar in concrete | Slows chloride ingress significantly |
| Rubber conveyor belts | Flexible, chemical-resistant surface layer |
| PVC piping | Good for brine transport; avoid UV exposure |

### Tier 3 — Moderate Durability

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Galvanized steel structures | Zinc layer eventually degrades in brine |
| Treated timber supports | Suitable for dry zones; fails in wet brine |
| Standard concrete | Requires sealant; cracks accelerate corrosion |
| Aluminum components | Pits quickly in saturated salt environments |

### Tier 4 — Poor Durability

| Item | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Carbon steel (uncoated) | Rapid rust in brine; requires heavy maintenance |
| Standard iron castings | High corrosion rate; short service life |
| Limestone aggregate | Dissolves slowly in saturated brine over time |

---

## Key Durability Factors

- **Brine concentration** — saturated brine is far more corrosive than dry rock salt dust
- **Temperature cycling** — freeze-thaw cycles crack coatings and concrete
- **Mechanical abrasion** — salt crystals grind surfaces during transport
- **Ventilation** — humid, enclosed mine air accelerates metal oxidation

---

## Maintenance Recommendations

1. Inspect Tier 3–4 components every 6 months for surface degradation.
2. Apply anti-corrosion coatings annually to exposed steel.
3. Replace rubber seals and gaskets on a 2-year cycle in wet areas.
4. Use FRP or HDPE where metal replacement costs are prohibitive.
