# Morning Rituals and Quiet Focus

The first light of morning spills across the kitchen counter while the kettle hums softly, releasing thin ribbons of steam that drift toward the glass above the sink. Settling into a chapter of a book before the workday begins helps quiet the mind and steadies curiosity for whatever tasks lie ahead. These modest, repeatable rituals remind us that attention is not a fixed trait but a gentle practice we can renew each morning with intention.
